Canonical English is checked against article evidence. Turkish completeness is guarded before publication; these checks do not claim translation accuracy. Historical measurements and legacy positive verdicts stay stored without becoming current quality claims.

quality/ · at publication · no model calls

Grounding flags: output numerals unsupported by the article text shown to the model.

Prompt word budgets and preferred-tag / singleton filter diagnostics.

Stored once, read as measured.

Retired output-shape proxies are no longer computed for publication or the default report. Readers ignore old extra keys. Missing values are shown honestly.

A short, grounded summary can still omit the news; content-floor checks address that separate risk.

2 · THE READER

wrong-story feedback · no positive-label chore

POST /verdict

One canonical summary ID in both languages. Wrong, reason and optional note. A bare repeat preserves existing annotations.

wrong_fact · not_news
duplicate · wrong_place

Each reason has an owner.

Facts → grounding. Not news → relevance. Repetition → dedupe. Wrong placement → editorial ranking.

Old positive rows remain historical. An unreported story is never treated as a positive example.

only wrong_fact can be compared
with the factual grounding judge

wrong-only examples do not establish
precision, specificity or calibration

3 · GROUNDING JUDGE

independent model · estimated and capped

ainews eval judge --bulletin latest
--sample 12 --max-cost 0.10

Sample published stories first, compare summary claims with attributable article text, and record every result with model, usage and prompt hash.

A suspect claim is an inspection target.

The judge checks factual support, not style, relevance or Turkish fluency. Known factual-error examples can show misses; they cannot establish overall calibrated quality.

Changing a prompt changes the measurement condition.

article evidence → factual claims
unsupported claim → inspect the story

unknown article provenance is identified
prompt hashes remain on recorded rows

4 · PRODUCTION AGREEMENT

recorded by the ordinary digest · no extra experiment

rank ×3 → bulletins.agreement

Three shuffled production readings, aggregated by Borda count.

min(mean Kendall τ,
chance-corrected Jaccard)

The standalone paid ranking probe is removed.

Order and membership can fail separately.

Agreement measures consistency, not human quality. Chance correction removes the overlap expected just from pool size. Low scores call for inspection; they do not refuse publication.

Fewer than two valid readings leave no measured agreement.

low agreement → inspect order and selection
agreement n/a → inspect failed rank calls

no missing score becomes a successful one

5 · PROMPT COMPARISON

same bodies, chosen model · pre-call cost guard

ainews eval corpus
ainews eval compare --prompt-b candidate.md

Freeze local article bodies. Run shipped and candidate prompts over identical inputs. Print measurements side by side and name no automatic winner.

Compare the writing instruction.

A different day's news is a confounder. Keep bodies and model fixed, then inspect factual, content and budget tradeoffs. Corpus bodies stay in ignored local data.

canonical English prompt
frozen attributable article text
same model for both candidates
explicit cost ceiling before calls

6 · REPORT

read recorded rows · append dated Markdown

ainews eval report --since 30d
--bulletin ID narrows detail only

Keep actionable checks, agreement, categorized wrong feedback and recorded product/eval spend.

Sum each digest Run cost once. Bulletin versions and cached language views do not duplicate spend.

Act on a concrete signal.

Unsupported facts → inspect grounding. Missing key facts → inspect the writing prompt. Phantom topic counts → inspect visible membership. Repeated duplicates → inspect dedupe. Low or absent agreement → inspect ranking. Unexpected spend → inspect paid steps and retries.
